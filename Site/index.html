About

RadicleBotany is a botanical education app for iOS built for learners who want to understand plants deeply.

Three identification modes let you learn at your own pace: Observe uses trait-based keying to teach you how botanists identify plants. Capture uses photo recognition for quick identification. Both combines the two: snap a photo, then verify through traits.

Every species entry includes morphological traits, family context, conservation status, and occurrence mapping. The goal is understanding.

Three Ways to Identify
Mode 1

Observe

Trait-based keying. Select leaf shape, flower color, growth habit, and other morphological traits to narrow down species. The same process botanists use in the field.

Mode 2

Capture

Photo identification powered by PlantNet. Snap a photo of a leaf, flower, fruit, or bark and get species matches with confidence scores.

Mode 3

Both

The hybrid approach. Start with a photo for a quick match, then verify through trait-based observation. The fastest path to confident identification.
